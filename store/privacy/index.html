SwipeStop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Developer: Invental Data Labs

SwipeStop (“the app”) helps you track and limit the short videos you watch on your Android phone. This policy explains what the app does with information.

1. Information the app uses

To count reels and shorts, the app uses Android’s Accessibility Service while Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat or Facebook is open. It looks only at the screen layout of those apps (view IDs, sizes and positions, and on Facebook the labels of the like, comment and share buttons) to know when a short-video player is on screen. It does not read your messages, what you type, or your contacts, and it does not record your screen.

2. What is stored

The app stores on your phone only: the time of each reel counted, which app it was in, time spent, your limits, schedules and settings.

3. What is shared

Nothing. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics. No information is sent to us or to anyone else.

4. Your control

You can turn off the Accessibility Service at any time in Android Settings. You can export your data as a CSV file or delete it in the app (Settings → Reset all data). Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
